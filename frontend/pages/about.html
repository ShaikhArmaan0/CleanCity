About CleanCity

A community-driven platform empowering citizens to take charge of civic cleanliness and hold local authorities accountable.

Our Mission

CleanCity was founded with a simple belief: every citizen deserves to live in a clean, healthy environment — and every citizen has the power to make that happen. We bridge the gap between communities and local authorities by making it effortless to report civic issues, track their resolution, and hold governments accountable through transparency.

We believe that when communities speak together, change happens faster. Our platform amplifies those voices, ensuring no complaint goes unheard.

How It Works

📸 1. Report

Spot a civic issue — garbage overflow, illegal dumping, broken infrastructure, drainage problem, or public hygiene violation? Report it in under 2 minutes with photos, your location, and a description.

🗳️ 2. Vote

Browse complaints from across the city. Upvote the most urgent ones to signal priority. The most voted complaints surface to the top, ensuring the worst problems get addressed first.

✅ 3. Track

Every complaint gets a unique ID. Track real-time status updates — Submitted → In Progress → Resolved. Get notified when your complaint moves forward. Earn cleanliness score points for civic participation.

Impact So Far

CleanCity has been designed to create measurable change in urban environments. Every report filed, every vote cast, and every resolved complaint contributes to a cleaner, healthier city for everyone.
