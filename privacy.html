    Privacy Policy

    How CleanCity collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

    📋 Terms & Conditions 🔒 Privacy Policy ← Back to Dashboard
    📥
    1. Information We Collect
    ▶

    When you use CleanCity, we collect the following types of information:

    • Account Information: Your name, phone number, and email address provided during registration.
    • Complaint Data: Reports you file, including description, location, category, images, and status history.
    • Activity Data: Votes cast, comments made, and engagement metrics that contribute to your CleanlinesScore.
    • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, and approximate location for security and analytics purposes.
    🔄
    2. How We Use Your Information
    ▶

    CleanCity uses your information to:

    • Operate the complaint reporting and tracking system.
    • Notify you via SMS or in-app notifications about updates to your complaints.
    • Assign complaints to the appropriate municipal authority zones.
    • Calculate and display your CleanlinesScore and civic engagement metrics.
    • Improve platform features and identify patterns in civic issues.
    • Ensure security and prevent fraud or abuse of the system.
    👁️
    3. Information Visibility
    ▶

    Some of your information is visible to other users of the platform:

    • Your name appears on complaints you file and comments you post.
    • Your CleanlinesScore may be displayed on public leaderboards.
    • Complaint descriptions and images you submit are visible to all users.

    Your phone number and email are never shown to other citizen users. They are only accessible to administrators for account management and support purposes.

    🤝
    4. Data Sharing with Authorities
    ▶

    CleanCity shares complaint information with designated municipal authority supervisors for the sole purpose of resolving civic issues. This includes:

    • Complaint description, location, category, and images.
    • Your name (for coordination purposes).
    • Contact details only when necessary for complaint resolution.

    We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

    🛡️
    5. Data Security
    ▶

    We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

    • All passwords are hashed using secure bcrypt algorithms and are never stored in plain text.
    • API access is protected using JWT (JSON Web Tokens) with short expiry periods.
    • Data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections.
    • Admin and citizen sessions are completely isolated.

    Despite these measures, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong, unique passwords and report any suspicious activity immediately.

    🧾
    6. Data Retention & Deletion
    ▶

    Your data is retained as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

    • Your personal profile information is permanently deleted.
    • Complaints you filed may be anonymized and retained for civic records.
    • Votes and comments may be removed or anonymized.

    To request deletion of your account and associated data, go to Profile → Change Password → Delete Account, or contact the platform administrator.

    ✉️
    7. Contact Us
    ▶

    If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the Contact page of the platform.

    We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 72 hours on business days.
